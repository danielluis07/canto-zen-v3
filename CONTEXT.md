# Canto Zen

Canto Zen is a fictional home-furniture brand and e-commerce experience. Its storefront combines furniture shopping with editorial imagery of inhabited rooms.

## Language

**Editorial opening**:
The homepage's introductory composition, which establishes the brand's mood and gives visitors a clear path into shopping.

**Asymmetric image pairing**:
A composition combining a larger room photograph with a smaller furniture or material-detail photograph, using unequal sizes and staggered placement.
